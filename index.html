Performance

Chinese zither (guzheng) and musical theater.

Chinese Zither — Guzheng

Classical & Contemporary Repertoire

The guzheng is a 21-string Chinese zither with over 2,500 years of history. I’ve studied it for several years, performing classical repertoire and contemporary arrangements. Playing guzheng connects me with my cultural heritage while challenging me to grow as a musician and interpreter. I received a Performance Award at the 2025 Stanford Baipu International Chinese Music Competition, and my performance recording of Liuyang River was selected for presentation at the 2025 Guangdong Guzheng Association Art Exhibition. I also perform with the California Youth Chinese Symphony, including at Los Altos Rotary’s Fine Art in the Park, and mentor younger musicians in the ensemble. Through performance and competition, I’ve learned how music can preserve tradition while bringing communities together.

Musical Theater

School & Summer Productions

Stage performance has helped me become a more confident storyteller and teammate. I played Hortensia in Matilda Jr. and Plankton in SpongeBob SquarePants: The Musical. My performance as Plankton earned me the “Best Evil Laugh” award. Taking on different characters challenged me to use my voice and physical expression to bring them to life and connect with the audience. Working with Mitty’s stage crew also showed me what happens behind the scenes and how design, teamwork, and problem-solving bring a production together. These experiences deepened my appreciation for storytelling and showed me how both performance and design can shape what an audience feels and experiences.

Community

Co-President

True Crime Club

Archbishop Mitty High School

As co-president, I helped revive a previously inactive club and shifted our focus toward the psychology and neuroscience behind criminal behavior. I introduced icebreaker games, small-group activities, and interactive discussions to make meetings more engaging and help members get to know each other. We explored offender motives, patterns of behavior, and real cases from different perspectives. Participation grew to around 30 students per meeting, with approximately 200 members in the club.

About

I enjoy exploring ideas through art, design, science, and technology. My interests range from visual storytelling and digital illustration to interaction design and biology-inspired experimentation.

I'm fascinated by how people experience the world — through stories, music, learning, and creativity — and I enjoy building projects that explore those experiences in new ways.

Outside of design and science, I study the guzheng, perform in musical theater, and enjoy experimenting with different artistic mediums including watercolor, ceramics, and digital painting.

I'm especially curious about how neuroscience, creative technology, and good design can intersect to build tools that genuinely help people think, feel, and connect.
