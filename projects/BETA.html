BETA

Climb together. Share your beta. Keep the crew going.

A mobile-first social climbing experience that turns a climbing day into an easy loop: capture the moment, share it with friends, stay connected, and climb again. Partner discovery is available when needed, never a prerequisite.

The opportunity

How might a climbing app create more time together, not more time scrolling?

Recreational climbers arrive in many ways: with friends, a spouse, family, a regular belay partner, or alone. Some boulder and never need a belayer. BETA starts with the climb rather than assuming everyone needs a match.

The product connects low-friction climb capture, shareable moments, private crews, optional adult partner discovery, and a preserved Route Lab for deeper reflection.

Climb → Capture → Share → Connect → Climb again.

The product loop

Useful for a crew of friends—and for someone who needs a partner tonight.

  1. 01

    Share a climb

    Upload a photo or short video, preview it, and turn it into a memorable card without creating a crew or annotating a route.

  2. 02

    Challenge friends

    Ask “How would you climb this?”, collect spoiler-safe predictions, and reveal different solutions without ranking bodies or styles.

  3. 03

    Keep a crew together

    Create a private crew, invite people through expiring links, and return to the people who make climbing fun.

  4. 04

    Find a partner, optionally

    Consenting adults can discover compatible climbers at the same gym when their existing crew is unavailable.

Design choices

Real-world connection over app engagement.

  • Three independent starting points: share a climb, climb with my crew, or find a partner.
  • Mobile-first capture with preview, confirm, discard, and private-by-default sharing.
  • No follower graph, infinite feed, rankings, or pressure to track grades.
  • English and Simplified Chinese UI with persistent language choice.
  • Installable PWA, future Capacitor path, and web links that work without installing an app.

Safety and architecture

Make trust part of the product.

  • Adult-only partner matching
  • Self-reported belay labels
  • Supabase Auth and RLS
  • Private media storage
  • Revocable crew invitations
  • Local drafts and labeled demo mode
