BETA

Climb together. Share your beta. Keep the crew going.

A bilingual, mobile-first climbing platform that connects route reading, memorable climbs, friendly beta challenges, private crews, and optional partner discovery in one experience.

The opportunity

How might a climbing app create more time together, not more time scrolling?

Recreational climbers arrive in many ways: with friends, a spouse, family, a regular belay partner, or alone. Some boulder and never need a belayer. BETA starts with the climb rather than assuming everyone needs a match.

The product connects route reading, low-friction climb capture, spoiler-safe challenges, private crews, and optional adult partner discovery without becoming another tracker, dating app, or endless social feed.

Read → Climb → Challenge → Connect → Climb again.

From seeing the climb to coming back for the people.

Social, without the feed

Make one climbing moment worth sending to the group chat.

01

My Climb

Turn a photo or short video into a polished card with an optional grade, gym, caption, and personal highlight.

02

Beta Challenge

Ask friends how they would climb a route, protect responses from spoilers, then reveal the crew's ideas together.

03

Different Beta

Compare multiple approaches side by side and celebrate clever, smooth, creative, or resilient movement without choosing a winner.

04

Crew Recap

Collect the moments a private crew chooses to share, then make planning the next climbing day the natural next action.

Design choices

Real-world connection over app engagement.

  • Four connected product areas: Route Lab, My Climbs, Challenge & Compare, and Connect.
  • Mobile-first capture with preview, confirm, discard, and private-by-default sharing.
  • No follower graph, infinite feed, rankings, or pressure to track grades.
  • Route Lab supports deeper prediction, comparison, and reflection without making detailed annotation mandatory.
  • English and Simplified Chinese UI, installable PWA support, and a future path to iOS and Android.

Safety and architecture

Make trust part of the product.

  • Adult-only partner matching
  • Self-reported belay labels
  • Supabase Auth and RLS
  • Private media storage
  • Revocable crew invitations
  • Local drafts and labeled demo mode
