Plasteroids

Plasteroids is a collaborative game created during Carnegie Mellon’s National High School Game Academy, combining original visual design, gameplay systems, and Unity development into a complete playable game.

The Project

The project was developed by a student team through an iterative game production process, moving from early concepts and prototypes to a finished playable experience.

My Role

I served as the producer and artist on a multidisciplinary team, working across visual development, game design, and production while collaborating with teammates on implementation and iteration.I helped organize the production process, tracked progress across disciplines, and kept the team aligned on deadlines so we could deliver a complete and enjoyable playable game on time.

From Unity to Web

After the program, I updated the Unity project and build configuration to adapt Plasteroids for browser play, making the final game directly accessible through my portfolio.
